### 📌 Projects & Research

**🔹 Bioacoustic Classification: Bird Species Identification**
* **Status:** **Published Research** (*IJRASET*).
* **The Work:** Explored the use of **Short-Time Fourier Transform (STFT)** and **AlexNet** to classify avian vocalizations. This project emphasized the importance of meticulous signal preprocessing in AI.
* **Methodology:** Engineered a pipeline to convert raw audio into spectrograms, bridging the gap between bioacoustics and computer vision.
* **Tech:** Python, TensorFlow, STFT, Librosa.

**🔹 Clinical Support: Automated Skin Lesion Classification**
* **Domain:** Medical AI / Healthcare Diagnostics.
* **The Work:** Implemented a classification system using **MobileNet** to assist in identifying dermatological features. 
* **Methodology:** Focused on high-sensitivity localized feature extraction. Utilized Data Augmentation to improve model robustness against varied clinical imagery.
* **Goal:** To understand how AI can better support clinical decision-making through reliable, data-driven diagnostic aid.
* **Tech:** Python, TensorFlow, Keras.

**🔹 Predictive Diagnostics: Heart Attack Risk Classification**
* **The Work:** Developed a diagnostic framework to predict heart disease risk using the BRFSS dataset, with a focus on overcoming severe data constraints.
* **Research Focus:** Addressed a **~9.4% class imbalance** through strategic random undersampling and performed advanced **feature engineering**, including the construction of **second-order interaction terms** to capture complex biological relationships.
* **Methodology:** Conducted a comparative study of **Logistic Regression, LDA, Naive Bayes, and KNN**. Optimized models to achieve an **F1-Score of 0.779 and an AUC of 0.849**, prioritizing recall to ensure early detection in clinical settings.
* **Tech:** Python, Scikit-learn, Statistical Modeling, Feature Engineering.

**🔹 Robustness Analysis: Credit Score & Risk Classification**
* **The Work:** Developed a multi-model classification framework to predict creditworthiness, focusing on model reliability and confidence scoring.
* **Research Focus:** Beyond standard accuracy, I explored **In-Distribution (ID) vs. Out-of-Distribution (OOD)** confidence analysis. This allowed me to study how models like **KNN, Random Forest, and XGBoost** behave when encountering unfamiliar data—a key step toward building trustworthy AI.
* **Methodology:** Handled complex data cleaning, feature engineering, and dimensionality reduction via **PCA**. Evaluated performance using AUC/ROC curves and KDE plots for confidence distribution.
* **Tech:** Python, Scikit-learn, XGBoost, Seaborn.
